The short version

Bible Grace has no account system. What you create in the app stays on your iPhone.

To find and fix problems, the app sends crash reports and anonymous usage statistics to Google’s Firebase. They are not linked to your name, your email or any account. See Crash reports and usage statistics.

The one thing you type that leaves the device is a question you ask on the Ask screen. It is sent to be answered and is not stored. See Asking a question.

We do not sell or share personal data, we do not use advertising software, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple and processed through your App Store account. We never see your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives purchase transaction information and a randomly generated identifier for your installation. That identifier is not your name or your email. Apple’s handling of your purchase is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Asking a question

When you ask a question on the Ask screen, the question is sent to our own server, which passes it to Anthropic to be answered. Anthropic processes it on our behalf and does not use it to train their models.

The request contains two things: your question, and the random identifier for your installation that we already use to check your subscription. It does not contain your name, your email, your reading history, your notes or anything else from the app.

We do not store your questions. Our server keeps one thing: a count of how many questions that installation has asked today, which exists to stop the service being abused and is discarded after two days. The question and the answer are kept on your iPhone so you can look back at them, and deleting the app removes them.

Do not type anything into Ask that you would not want to send over the internet. The answers are produced by software, may be wrong, and are not counsel from a minister, a doctor or a lawyer. If you ask something that suggests you are in danger, the app replies with a crisis helpline instead of asking the model anything.

Crash reports and usage statistics

The app uses two parts of Google’s Firebase:

  • Crashlytics. If the app crashes, it sends a crash report: where in the app’s code the crash happened, the app and iOS versions, and the device model. We use these to fix the crash.
  • Analytics. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, such as which screens are opened, how long the app is used for, whether onboarding was finished and the choices made there (the church tradition you picked, if any, and your daily reading goal), which Bible chapters are opened and in which translation, the reference of a verse you save or highlight (never your notes), reading milestones reached, when a subscription offer was shown and when one was bought (the product, never your payment details), that a question was asked (never what it said), the app and iOS versions, the device model, and an approximate region that Google works out from the internet connection. We use these to understand which parts of the app work and which need improving.

Both are tied only to a random identifier that Firebase creates for this installation of the app. It is not your name, your email or the advertising identifier, and deleting the app discards it. We do not use this information for advertising, and it is not used to track you. Google processes it on our behalf under Google’s privacy policy.

Removing your data

  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.
  • We hold no account or profile data about you, and we do not store your questions. If you would like a subscription record, crash reports or usage statistics removed, contact us and we will pass the request to RevenueCat or Google.

Children

Bible Grace is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
